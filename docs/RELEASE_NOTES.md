## Continuity Keeper 2.4.0 for Codex Plus

- Saves progress and hands off at **≤5% remaining in the five-hour window** or **≤2% in the weekly window**.
- Detects windows by duration and preserves the 5% fallback for other or missing durations.
- Healthy quota checks remain silent, and unknown notices are rate limited.
- Includes the installer, recovery demo, and reproducible release package.
